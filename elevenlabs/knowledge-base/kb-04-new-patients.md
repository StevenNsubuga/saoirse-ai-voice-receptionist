# New Patients — Riverside Dental

**Are we accepting new patients?** Yes, we are currently accepting new patients, both private and PRSI.

**First visit:** A 40-minute new patient examination (€75) including any necessary X-rays, a full mouth assessment, and a written treatment plan if work is needed. No treatment is done at the first visit unless urgent.

**What to bring:** A list of any medications you take, and your PPS number if you wish to use your PRSI entitlement — you can give your PPS number over the phone when booking, or at reception on the day.

**Previous records:** If you're transferring from another practice, we can request your records and X-rays — just ask at your first visit.

**Nervous patients:** Very welcome. Mention it when booking and we'll allow extra time. Dr. Byrne has a special interest in anxious patients.
