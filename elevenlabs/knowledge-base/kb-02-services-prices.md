# Services & Prices — Riverside Dental

All prices in euro. Prices are a guide; complex cases quoted after examination.

**Examinations**
- Dental examination: €60
- New patient examination (includes X-rays): €75
- Child examination (under 16): €40
- Emergency appointment (same-day assessment): €95, treatment cost additional

**Hygiene**
- Scale and polish (hygienist): €70
- Exam plus scale and polish combined: €110

**Treatments**
- Filling (white/composite): €120 – €180 depending on size
- Tooth extraction: €120 – €200
- Root canal treatment: €400 (front tooth) – €700 (molar)
- Crown (porcelain): €700 – €950
- Teeth whitening (take-home kit): €350
- Mouthguard / nightguard: €150

**Not offered:** orthodontics (braces/Invisalign) and dental implants — we refer patients to specialist colleagues.

**Payment:** card, cash, or bank transfer. Payment is due on the day of treatment. Treatment plans over €500 can be split across visits.
