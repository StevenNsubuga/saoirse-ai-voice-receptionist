# Appointment Policy — Riverside Dental

**Cancellations:** We ask for at least 48 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule. Missed appointments or cancellations with less than 48 hours' notice may incur a €40 fee, as the time is reserved for you.

**Running late:** If you arrive more than 10 minutes late, we may need to shorten or rebook your appointment.

**Appointment lengths:** Standard check-up 20 minutes. New patient examination 40 minutes. Hygienist 30 minutes. Treatment appointments vary and are advised at booking.

**Deposits:** Cosmetic treatments (whitening, crowns) require a €100 booking deposit, deducted from the final cost.

**Reminders:** We call or text a reminder the day before your appointment. If your appointment no longer suits, please tell us during that reminder call and we'll rebook you.

**Children:** Under-16s must be accompanied by a parent or guardian.
