# PRSI, Medical Cards & Tax Relief — Riverside Dental

**PRSI Treatment Benefit Scheme:** If you have enough PRSI contributions, you are entitled each calendar year to a free dental examination, and a subsidy toward a scale and polish so you pay no more than €15 for it. We can take your PPS number and date of birth over the phone when you book, or at reception, and we confirm your entitlement with the Department of Social Protection before your appointment. Most employed and self-employed people qualify.

**Medical cards (DTSS):** We accept a limited number of medical card patients. Medical card holders are entitled to a free examination and certain treatments under the scheme. Availability of medical card places varies — the receptionist can confirm current availability, so we'll log a callback for you.

**Tax relief:** Non-routine treatments (crowns, root canals, etc.) qualify for 20% tax relief. We provide a completed Med 2 form after treatment for your Revenue claim. Routine treatment (exams, cleanings, fillings, extractions) does not qualify.
