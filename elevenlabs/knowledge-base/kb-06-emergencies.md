# Dental Emergencies — Riverside Dental

**Same-day emergency slots:** We hold emergency slots each weekday morning for severe pain, swelling, broken teeth, or knocked-out teeth. Emergency assessment costs €95; treatment is additional. Ring as early as possible — slots go quickly.

**What counts as an emergency:** severe or worsening toothache, facial or gum swelling, bleeding that won't stop, a knocked-out or badly broken adult tooth, an abscess, or trauma to the mouth.

**Serious warning signs:** If there is swelling affecting breathing or swallowing, or a serious facial injury, this is a medical emergency — contact emergency services on 112 or go to the nearest emergency department. Do not wait for a dental appointment.

**Out of hours:** The practice is closed evenings, Sundays, and bank holidays. Out of hours, patients can contact a dentist through the Dublin Dental Hospital's emergency service or ring us first thing the next working day — we'll always try to fit emergencies in.
